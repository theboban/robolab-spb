01 · Curriculum

What Kids Will Learn

Every week, students leave with a working robot and a new idea they invented themselves.

Robot Building

Snappy, sturdy machines built from LEGO Education WeDo 2.0 and beginner-friendly Arduino kits — gears, motors, sensors and all.

Programming Basics

Kids start with drag-and-drop block coding, then graduate to simple C++ & Python sketches that make their robots move, sense and react.

Engineering Thinking

Test it, break it, fix it. Students learn the real design loop: hypothesize → prototype → measure → improve — with rulers, not vibes.

Creativity & Problem Solving

Open-ended challenges — “design a robot that cleans this maze” — turn curiosity into confidence and teamwork.
